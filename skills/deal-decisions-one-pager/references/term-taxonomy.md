# Term taxonomy

Group every extracted decision under one of these terms. A fixed taxonomy means the page has the same shape each time it is rebuilt, and it makes gaps visible: an empty row is a question nobody has answered yet.

## Pricing and commercial
- List price and pricing model (per seat, per usage, platform fee)
- Discount floor (the number below which someone senior must approve)
- Discount by term length (annual, multi-year, prepay)
- Pilot and proof-of-concept pricing, including whether pilots are ever free
- Ramp deals and deferred start dates
- Payment terms (Net 30, Net 45, Net 60) and who can extend them
- Price increase cap on renewal
- Overage rates and how overage is billed
- Currency and who absorbs FX
- Reseller, partner, and referral pricing

## Contract terms
- Minimum contract term
- Auto-renewal and notice period
- Termination for convenience: offered, and on what conditions
- Liability cap, and the standard carve-outs
- Indemnity positions
- Governing law and venue the company will accept
- Insurance coverage the company carries
- Whether the company will sign buyer paper at all, and above what deal size

## Security and compliance
- Certifications held, and expected dates for those in progress
- Security exceptions previously granted, and to whom
- Data residency commitments the company will make
- Deletion and retention periods
- Subprocessor approval and notice
- Audit rights offered
- Penetration test sharing: summary, full report, or under NDA
- Custom security requirements previously accepted

## Product and delivery
- Roadmap commitments made in writing, and to whom
- Custom development: offered, and at what price
- Integration commitments
- SLA offered, and whether service credits attach
- Support tiers and response times
- Onboarding and implementation scope, and whether it is charged
- Training included

## Process and authority
- Who approves discounts, at each band
- Who signs contracts
- Who can commit to a roadmap item
- Who owns security review responses
- Who approves a non-standard payment term
- What triggers legal review

## Gaps worth naming explicitly

When these have no decision behind them, say so. Each one is a predictable future emergency:

- The largest discount the company will ever approve
- Whether the company signs buyer paper, and above what size
- The lowest liability cap the company will accept
- What happens when a buyer demands a certification the company does not hold
- Who decides when the founder is unreachable
